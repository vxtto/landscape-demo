What exactly do these metrics mean?

Usage composite averages the model’s percentile inside OpenRouter and ZenMux. Missing a platform Top 50 contributes zero; it does not prove zero usage.

AAI is the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index for models matched to the public snapshot. No badge means “not publicly matched”, not a score of zero.

HF ecosystem score is only for open-weight models: 75% 30-day download percentile + 25% likes percentile. It is not compared with API-only models.

Open weights means an official public weight repository was resolved. It does not automatically mean open training data, unrestricted redistribution or OSI-defined Open Source AI.
